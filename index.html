Project

02 · Nov 2025 – Mar 2026

AI / ML · Forecasting · Python · Streamlit · MySQL

AI-ML driven forecasting for price prediction and yield optimization of agri-horticulture commodities

The problem

Farmers and traders decide what to grow and when to sell with little idea of where prices are heading. Prices for crops like arecanut swing year to year, and a bad guess costs real money.

What it does

You pick a commodity and the app shows its historical price trend, then forecasts future prices and yields — so you can see where the market is likely going before planting or selling.

What I did

  • Built core features and the database layer with SQL / MySQL.
  • Cleaned and processed the data with Pandas and NumPy.
  • Trained forecasting models with scikit-learn, XGBoost and ARIMA.
  • Plotted trends with Matplotlib and Seaborn.
  • Tested and debugged for reliability, then deployed it on Streamlit.

The detail

Two model families in one project: ARIMA, a classic time-series model, alongside XGBoost, a gradient-boosted model — so predictions can be compared rather than trusted blindly.

01 · DATA
